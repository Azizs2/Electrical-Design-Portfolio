## 📂 Featured Project Samples

### 1. 150kV Substation Single Line Diagram (SLD)
* **Scope:** High-voltage digital substation architecture comprising 5 primary bays (2x Line Bays, 2x Transformer Bays, and 1x Bus Coupler Bay with dual bus bar systems - Main 1 & Main 2).
* **Key Elements:** Detailed CT/VT data mapping schedules (ratio, burden, knee-point voltage, class accuracy), interlocking logic, and protection schemes.

### 2. Indoor Panel General Arrangement (GA) & Internal Component Layout
* **Scope:** Mechanical layout and structural design for indoor electrical enclosures (e.g., 150kV Energy Meter Panels).
* **Key Elements:** Front/rear views, base ground fixing, modular space allocation (Spare spaces), climate control integration (heating resistors & thermostats), and complete Bill of Materials (BOM) traceability.

### 3. Cable List & Interconnection Diagrams
* **Scope:** Control and instrumentation cable routing mapping from external sources (such as Marshaling Kiosks and Circuit Breakers) into main control panels.
* **Key Elements:** Precise cable specifications (e.g., $4 \times 6\text{ mm}^2$ for CT, $4 \times 4\text{ mm}^2$ for VT) and core numbering to prevent wiring errors.
