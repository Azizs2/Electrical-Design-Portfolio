# Electrical-Design-Portfolio
Technical documentation of high-voltage substation designs, schematic architectures, panel layouts, and industrial wiring systems.

# 150kV Substation Single Line Diagram (SLD) - GIS System

Designed a high-voltage digital substation architecture for a 150kV Gas Insulated Switchgear (GIS) system in strict compliance with IEC and EGAT international engineering standards. The layout features a 5-bay configuration (2x Line Bays, 2x Transformer Bays, and 1x Bus Coupler Bay) operating on a dual busbar arrangement (Main 1 & Main 2). Integrated comprehensive CT/VT core schedules mapping knee-point voltage ($V_k$), secondary resistance ($R_{ct}$), burden, and class accuracy for tariff metering, zone bus protection, and backup protection schemes.

**Technologies & Standards Used**: Single Line Diagrams (SLD), GIS Technology, IEC Standards, EGAT Compliance, Protection Interlocking, AutoCAD, Engineering Base.

# 150kV Substation Single Line Diagram (SLD) - AIS System

Engineered an outdoor Air Insulated Switchgear (AIS) single line diagram tailored for high-capacity power transmission line termination and bay isolation. Designed clear phase-to-phase and phase-to-earth clearance architecture, primary equipment layout (Disconnector Switches, Earth Switches, Circuit Breakers), and neutral grounding schemes to optimize outdoor operational safety, maintenance access, and atmospheric exposure resilience.

**Technologies & Standards Used**: Air Insulated Switchgear (AIS), Outdoor Substation Design, Clearances & Insulation Mapping, IEC 61936, AutoCAD.

# Indoor Panel General Arrangement (GA) & Mechanical Enclosure Layout

Engineered mechanical enclosure architecture and internal component placement for 150kV indoor control and energy meter panels. Features dual door configuration (acrylic viewing / solid metal), 2mm enclosure thickness (RAL 7032 finish), bottom cable gland entry plates, and integrated climate control (heating resistors & thermostats). Fully mapped Bill of Materials (BOM) with functional mark allocations (ION 8650 energy meters, CCEVA/TCEVA test blocks, and MCB protection).

**Technologies & Standards Used**: Panel General Arrangement (GA), Mechanical Layout, Bill of Materials (BOM), Thermal & Climate Management, AutoCAD.

# Control & Instrumentation Interconnection Cable Schedule

Structured comprehensive inter-panel wiring schedules and external cable routing diagrams connecting Marshaling Kiosks ($=\text{E0X}+\text{S2}$) and Circuit Breakers (GCB) to main energy meter panels ($=\text{E0X}+\text{WS1}$). Defined precise conductor cross-sections ($4\times6\text{ mm}^2$ for CT secondary circuits, $4\times4\text{ mm}^2$ for VT potential circuits) with core-by-core identification to ensure seamless field termination and zero wiring mismatch.

**Technologies & Standards Used**: Cable Interconnection Diagrams, Wire Schedule Mapping, CT/VT Wiring Schemes, Substation Secondary Systems.
